🏦 Bank Loan Analysis Dashboard
---

This Power BI project analyzes bank loan applications, approvals, funding, and customer insights to understand lending performance and risk factors.

📊 Key Insights:
---
- Total loan applications and approval/rejection rates
- Funded vs received amount analysis
- Customer segmentation based on income, credit score, and employment
- Loan status distribution (Approved vs Rejected)
- Time-based trend analysis of loan applications
- Key factors affecting loan approval decisions

📈 Key KPIs:
---
- Total Applications
- Total Funded Amount
- Total Received Amount
- Approval Rate
- Approved vs Rejected Loans

🛠 Tools & Technologies:
---
- Power BI
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Visualization
